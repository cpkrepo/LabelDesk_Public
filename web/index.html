    Printers

    A printer that gets paused (switched off, cable out) is resumed by itself once it answers again (Fedora and Mac; on Windows press Resume).

    Printers on the network

    LabelDesk looks for the DYMO printers by itself when it starts and every few minutes: it sets up a printer it finds for the first time and follows one whose address changed. Other DYMO printers it finds are listed here to choose from.

      Tag logo

      Your shop's logo, bottom right of the built-in tag (PNG or JPEG; black and white prints best). It's kept on this PC only.

      DYMO printers by IP

      If DYMO Connect or Windows can't find the printers (often on Wi-Fi), check them here first; the check changes nothing. Add only works when the check says tcp 9100: open. Needs an administrator.
