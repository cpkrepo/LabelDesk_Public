Subject: LabelDesk – new way to print our DYMO labels

Hi all,

LabelDesk is a simple app for printing our DYMO labels: inventory tags on the 550 Turbo and 4x6 shipping
labels on the 5XL (paste a screenshot or drop the UPS/FedEx PDF – it finds the label, turns it upright and
sizes it). What you see on screen is exactly what prints.

It runs entirely on your own PC and prints straight to the printers – no server, and nothing about your labels is sent anywhere.

Download:
[download link]

- Windows: download LabelDesk-0.5.0-Windows.zip (DYMO Connect must already be installed – it uses the same driver).
- Fedora: download LabelDesk-0.5.0-Fedora.zip to your Downloads folder.

Each zip has a "How to install" file inside; it takes a couple of minutes. Let me know if anything doesn't print.
